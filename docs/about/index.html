A considered approach.
A wider perspective.

Literary representation built around the author, the work, and its long-term potential.

Mike Petro is an independent literary and film rights agent who works with authors to position their manuscripts for publishing, rights, and broader marketplace opportunities. He takes a hands-on approach to helping a project find its direction.

His work includes manuscript positioning, publishing strategy, author promotion, visibility development, bookstore opportunities, rights evaluation, and negotiations surrounding appropriate publishing and subsidiary-rights opportunities.
