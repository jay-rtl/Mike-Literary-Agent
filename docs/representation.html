Every compelling book begins with an author and an idea. What follows calls for a clear sense of audience, thoughtful positioning, and a strategy specific to the work.

01

Position the work.

Manuscript and project evaluation, market positioning, query preparation, and submission strategy. A distinctive premise and an identifiable readership provide the starting point.

MANUSCRIPT POSITIONING / AUTHOR DEVELOPMENT
02

Traditional publishing submissions and publisher communications, with attention to editorial interests, readership, and the right home for the project.

TRADITIONAL PUBLISHING / SUBMISSIONS
