INDEPENDENT REPRESENTATION

Clear agreements.
Informed decisions.

A professional relationship begins with the author, the work, and a shared understanding of the terms.

Mike Petro is an independent literary and film rights agent. The framework below sets out the matters to clarify when discussing representation, publishing strategy, and the handling of literary rights.

01

Written scope & authority

Identify the work covered, the services proposed, the rights to be handled, and the authority granted through a written representation agreement.

02

Transparent terms

Discuss compensation, commission, any service terms, responsibilities, duration, and termination provisions before entering an agreement.

03

Rights & author approval

Establish which rights the author controls, review relevant existing agreements, and clarify the approvals needed before material publishing or rights commitments.

04

Confidential materials

Agree how manuscripts, project details, and other confidential materials may be shared for evaluation, submissions, and representation discussions.

05

Documented communication

Keep a clear record of agreed terms, submissions, relevant correspondence, and negotiations so the work and the decisions surrounding it can be followed.

06

Realistic expectations

Representation, review, or submission does not guarantee publication, licensing, film or television production, or commercial success. Third parties make their own acquisition and development decisions.
