About the practice

Independent thinking. Author-centered guidance.

Every manuscript begins with a person, a distinct creative voice, and a set of goals that deserve to be understood before any rights are offered.

Mike Petro is presented as an independent literary and film rights agent. The practice centers on careful evaluation, clear communication, responsible submission strategies, and informed discussion of available rights. Specific services, responsibilities, and compensation are established in writing before representation begins.

Authors retain ownership of their intellectual property, subject to any rights they voluntarily license or assign in written contracts.

The guiding principle

“Protect the author's work. Clarify the opportunity. Respect the decision.”

Professional conduct

Transparency is part of the service.

Clear expectations matter just as much as strong creative work. These statements describe the intended standards of this website and should reflect actual business practices before launch.

Fees and compensation

Literary representation terms and any commission are disclosed in a written agreement. Reading or evaluation fee is advertised or required for considering representation. Any optional, separately priced services must be described in writing, must not be a condition of representation, and must not suggest a guaranteed outcome.

Conflicts and referrals

Material conflicts and relevant financial interests are to be disclosed. No paid recommendation or referral is presented as independent advice. Authors should be free to seek independent professional guidance, unless the author's literary works have been recommended, evaluated and endorsed by Inhouse Critic Reviewers to Independent Literary Agent.

Truthful results and testimonials

Only documented client work and genuine testimonials shared with permission may be used. Any material relationship affecting an endorsement will be disclosed. Illustrative publisher examples must never be mistaken for placements.

Consent and confidentiality

Inquiries are treated with care; sensitive material should not be sent until requested. Manuscripts and personal information should not be shared with outside parties without appropriate authorization, except when required by law.

Contracts and rights

Each proposal should identify which rights are being discussed, by whom and for what purpose. Publishing, screen, and subsidiary rights decisions should be documented, and legal questions referred to qualified counsel.

Accessible and respectful service

Clear language, practical communication, accessible site controls, and respectful evaluation regardless of a writer's background are part of the intended experience. Placement, sale or response time is guaranteed after all requirements have been successfully and diligently followed.
